# Introduction

## What is a Policy Violation?

Alauda Security Service lets you view, investigate, and address policy violations in your clusters. Violations are the practical output of policy evaluation and show where a build, deployment, or runtime action matches an enabled rule.

## How Violations Are Detected

Alauda Security Service policies detect a wide range of security issues, including:
- Vulnerabilities (CVEs)
- Violations of DevOps best practices
- High-risk build and deployment activities
- Suspicious runtime behaviors

You can use the default security policies or define your own custom policies. When an enabled policy is violated, Alauda Security Service reports it as a violation for your review and remediation.
